v8.1 sniper state machineseven ideas · separately scored
1 · Regime200 ticks classify BULL, BEAR, or NEUTRAL from slope, directional efficiency, net movement, and swing structure.
2 · Confirm80 ticks must agree. A pullback can disagree temporarily, but no entry occurs until alignment returns.
3 · VetoCHOP or dead/chaotic volatility blocks participation even if a slope exists.
4 · Enter20 ticks must form HL + bullish resumption for CALL, or LH + bearish resumption for PUT.
5 · MilkAfter expiry, wait for a fresh pullback in the same session and hunt another aligned entry.
6 · InvalidatePersistent opposite pressure sends the strategy to NEUTRAL first. It never flips BULL↔BEAR on one wiggle.
